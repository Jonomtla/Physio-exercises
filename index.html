Daily Exercises

Terminal Knee Extensions

3 sets × 15-20 reps each leg
▼

Setup: Loop a resistance band around a sturdy object at knee height. Step into the band so it's behind your knee.

Movement: Stand on the banded leg, slightly bend the knee, then fully straighten it against the band resistance. Focus on squeezing the quad at the top.

Tip: Keep your hips stable and avoid leaning back.

Single-Leg Glute Bridges

3 sets × 12 each side
▼

Setup: Lie on your back, knees bent, feet flat on the floor. Extend one leg straight out.

Movement: Press through the grounded foot to lift your hips until your body forms a straight line from shoulders to knee. Lower with control.

Tip: Don't let your hips drop or rotate. Squeeze your glute at the top.

Banded Clamshells

3 sets × 15 each side
▼

Setup: Lie on your side with a band around both thighs just above the knees. Bend knees to 90 degrees, feet together.

Movement: Keeping feet together, rotate your top knee up toward the ceiling like a clamshell opening. Lower with control.

Tip: Don't let your hips roll backward. Keep your core engaged.

Wall Sits

3 × 30-45 seconds
▼

Setup: Stand with your back against a wall, feet shoulder-width apart and about 2 feet from the wall.

Movement: Slide down until your thighs are parallel to the floor. Hold the position.

Important: Bias weight to your LEFT leg to strengthen that side more.

Tip: Keep your back flat against the wall and don't let your knees go past your toes.

Pre-Run Warmup

Only do these before running

Leg Swings

10 each direction, each leg
▼

Setup: Stand next to a wall or hold onto something sturdy for balance.

Forward/Back: Swing one leg forward and backward in a controlled pendulum motion. Keep your torso stable.

Side-to-Side: Face the wall and swing your leg across your body and out to the side.

Tip: Start with small swings and gradually increase the range of motion.
